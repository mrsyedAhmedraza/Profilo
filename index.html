Automatic Water Distribution System

Enhances water supply management using an Arduino, a level sensor, a DC water pump, and a liter meter. The system runs automatically in the field — when the level sensor detects a full tank, it switches the motor off, while the liter meter tracks water consumption. This helps water supply departments measure usage, plan tank requirements by day or month, and cut down on energy costs.
